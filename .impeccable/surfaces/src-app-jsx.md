## Scope and mode

Homepage redesign in **Persuade** mode, implemented as a React single-page experience.

## Audience, job, and action

Travelers and specialty-coffee learners should understand Montanoa as a real family farm and choose one of three actions: tour the farm, book a stay, or explore the Academy.

## Proof and content

Use only the family history, sustainable practices, Academy, lodging, location, and imagery documented on Montanoa's live site. The hands-and-cherries photograph is the opening anchor; Google Veo supplies the photographic transition footage.

## Constraints

Exact name: Montanoa. Desktop plus mobile beta. Scroll position is the source of truth. Essential content remains accessible without motion. Mobile uses center-safe composition and lighter encodes. Do not fabricate booking availability, prices, reviews, or business claims.

## Chosen direction

**The Living Coffee Lens:** a full-bleed documentary camera journey with edge-set field notes, sight lines, and a chapter rail. The memorable moment is the opening push through dried coffee cherries into the misty living farm.

## Unresolved

Final Veo clip files, booking destination URLs, and whether the mobile hero needs a separate 9:16 generation. Typography is resolved with self-hosted open-source Newsreader and Manrope.
