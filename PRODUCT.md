# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is people evaluating a visit to Monteverde who want an authentic coffee-farm experience, nature lodging, or both. Specialty-coffee learners and professionals are a secondary audience for Montanoa's Academy. These audience priorities are inferred from Montanoa's current site and the six approved story scenes.

## Product Purpose

The site introduces Montanoa as a family-run coffee village in San Luis, Monteverde and helps visitors choose a next step: tour the working farm, stay on the property, or explore the Academy. Success means the story is understood quickly and those three paths remain obvious throughout the experience.

## Positioning

Montanoa connects one family's lived transformation of a windswept cattle field into a biodiverse coffee farm with seed-to-cup education and a stay inside the landscape where the coffee grows.

## Operating Context

Visitors often evaluate the experience on travel-planning devices, including phones with variable network and decoding performance. The site uses a reversible, scroll-controlled cinematic sequence rather than autoplay as its main storytelling mechanism.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React implementation.
- Six approved chapters: The Golden Bean, Into the Farm, Grown With the Forest, From Cherry to Cup, Knowledge That Unites, and Stay Where It Begins.
- Google Flow / Veo supplies the photographic transition footage.
- Desktop is the native cinematic experience; mobile is a beta with lighter encodes, center-safe crops, safe-area layout, and hardened video seeking.
- Essential text and calls to action remain semantic HTML rather than being baked into imagery.
- Reduced-motion visitors receive a static, normally flowing version of the story.
- The exact business name is **Montanoa**, without an accent or the letter n with tilde.

## Brand Commitments

- Display name: Montanoa.
- Existing phrase: "In coffea veritas."
- Existing subject matter: family coffee farm, biodiversity, specialty coffee, Academy, lodging, and Monteverde.
- Preserve the supplied hands-and-coffee image as the opening visual truth.
- Tone: honest, grounded, knowledgeable, welcoming, and never resort-like or artificially luxurious.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the real farm, not generic coffee culture.
- Let imagery create wonder while copy stays factual and legible.
- Keep touring, learning, and staying equally discoverable.
- Make every scroll position reversible and reproducible.
- Protect the story on slow phones and for reduced-motion visitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard focus, semantic reading order, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, static media fallbacks, safe-area spacing, and non-video access to all narrative content are required.
